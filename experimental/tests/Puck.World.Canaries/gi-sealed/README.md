# Closed planar and curved rooms

The two black rooms have 25 mm walls around identical white receivers. A
unit-emission outer sphere lights every unoccluded outward direction. Neither
explicit lights nor sky, fill, feedback, AO or temporal history contributes.
The closed rooms therefore receive zero emission; the companion omits both
partitions and must receive the exterior source.

The camera selects each room through existing Int state, keeping the program
unchanged. Each observation waits for its current fenced cache, requires a
resolved receiver and reads its emission plus the independent field reference.
The saved images must be black inside both closed rooms and nonblack with
partitions absent. This scene pairs physical opposite observations with the
separate partition/proof shader withholding; removing geometry alone does not
prove that structural red leg.
