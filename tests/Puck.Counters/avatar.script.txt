# The seated-avatar workload script: the debug world as played, its Moth avatar seated before the default third-person
# camera, at the shipped high preset with temporal reuse and the indirect cache off, so every frame marches every lit
# pixel's shadows afresh and every field pass walks the avatar's pool instances. Its ceilings hold each pass's shape
# evaluations and march steps, which an avatar whose instances lose their distance certificates multiplies.
world.cadence off
world.quality high
world.temporal off
world.indirect off
world.shadow-mask exact
world.shadow-march exact
world.rate pause
world.wait ready 180
world.rate resume
world.wait 30
world.counters --json
