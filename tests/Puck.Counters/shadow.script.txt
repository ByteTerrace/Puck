# The shadow workload script: the debug world's one wide key light over its ground, avatar and cubes at the medium
# preset, with temporal reuse and indirect light off so every frame rasterizes its shadow cascades and lights every
# pixel afresh. Its counts hold the cascades' raster work and the surface pass's shadow reads.
world.cadence off
world.quality medium
world.temporal off
world.indirect off
world.rate pause
world.wait ready 180
world.rate resume
world.wait 120
world.counters --json
