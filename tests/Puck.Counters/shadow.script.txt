# The shadow workload script: the debug world's one wide soft-shadowed key light over its ground, avatar and cubes at
# the medium preset's exact shadow march, with temporal reuse and the indirect cache off so every frame marches every
# lit pixel afresh. Its ceilings hold the shadow pass's shape evaluations and march steps, which a field walk that
# stops rejecting far candidates multiplies.
world.cadence off
world.quality medium
world.temporal off
world.indirect off
world.shadow-mask exact
world.shadow-march exact
world.rate pause
world.wait ready 180
world.rate resume
world.wait 120
world.counters --json
