physical Sky category is enabled. Certified cache exits transport that source;
black wall hits contribute nothing, and feedback is disabled.

stays within two. The companion explicitly disables the Sky source, restoring
the harmonic term; both its images match that reference and its cached Sky

unoccluded ambient light.
