The two image legs use the running World and the ordinary destination observation route.
The lobby's white emissive beacon distinguishes a fixed destination anchor from a camera
translated into that destination. Turning the consumer changes its rays. Removing and
reopening the layer changes and restores the image. The blue Far sphere is 200 units away,
beyond the root's 100-unit march, and renders through a cone of 0.15 radians in its own
instance. Pixels around it must keep the destination sky. A nine-view edit must refuse
without replacing the installed image. The opposite leg deliberately moves the destination
anchor with the consumer and changes only the translation agreement.

views at one-eighth scale. They are not executed by the two image legs. The present
`world.budget` command does not expose the binder's live infinity plan; its count/cap
formatter still needs command wiring before a transcript can assert that count.

grant. The authored image bounds, frame settlement and cap companion have not run yet.
