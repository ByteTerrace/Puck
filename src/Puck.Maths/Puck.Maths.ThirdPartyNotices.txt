Puck.Maths includes a managed adaptation of Gourdon's decomposition described by
primecount's src/gourdon/pi_gourdon.cpp, Sigma.cpp, Phi0.cpp, AC.cpp,
AC_default.hpp, D.cpp and D_default.hpp. It specializes z=y and uses the
streamed semiprime correction described by src/P2.cpp in place of B after
algebraic cancellation with Sigma0. The cutoff and corrected x-star bounds
adapt src/util.cpp, with local storage bounds. The compressed prime-count
lookup follows src/PiTable.hpp; the segmented lookup and reflected easy leaves
follow src/gourdon/SegmentedPiTable.cpp and doc/Easy-Special-Leaves.pdf.
Vector filtering follows the approach described in
doc/Hard-Special-Leaves-SIMD-Filtering.pdf and src/gourdon/D_avx512.hpp,
with local packed-factor masks and descending set-bit extraction.
The retained small-prime partial sieve and compact factor helpers originate
from the earlier adaptation of src/lmo/pi_lmo5.cpp.
