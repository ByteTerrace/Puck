## Preserve the character

Keep warm brown skin, expressive brown eyes, black swept hair and one thick braid on her right side. The braid can trail during movement; its attachment does not change. The hood has an ivory face rim and lilac shell. Its opening follows the forehead and cheeks with a continuous shaped curve. Avoid a box or pentagonal house surrounding the face.

Armor combines broad plate faces, tapered volumes, controlled bevels and purposeful curves. Swept overlapping shoulders, large gauntlets, flared shin armor and substantial flat-soled boots establish the silhouette. Charcoal joints separate rigid pieces and provide bending space. Ivory and lilac dominate; muted ochre and restrained cyan lights are accents. Preserve the friendly arcade character and compact chibi proportions instead of adding realistic anatomy, surface noise or excessive effects.

## Interpret the proposed motion

The owner selected a nimble body with wings that visibly carry weight and serve a function. Let the body initiate takeoff and turns; let the wing assembly deploy, steer and settle with deliberate follow-through. Land through the boots, knees and hips, then stow the wings after the body stabilizes. The dramatic hand-supported landing is one possible key pose, not a mandatory landing on every contact.

The three-segment fan per side, linkage arrangement, outlet placement and approximate span in sheet 3 are design proposals extending the source's folded pack. They are not owner-approved engineering dimensions. Establish one consistent mechanism across all poses; do not generate extra segments during deployment. Use the pose sheet for gesture, not to override the mechanism with incidental panel-to-panel differences.

## Resolve differences deliberately

The owner source and subsequent owner feedback take precedence. Sheet 1 anchors overall identity and costume; sheet 2 clarifies surface treatment and expression. Sheets 3–4 explore mechanics and movement. Generated perspective views are not exact orthographic blueprints. Small seams, hinge details and outlet counts vary; reconcile those against the chosen mechanism rather than modeling every discrepancy. Incidental slogans on the sheets are not game copy, lore or implementation requirements.

For implementation priorities, use the [hero brief](../../armored-chibi-hero-brief.md). The [studio guide](../../../src/Puck.World/Assets/worlds/moth.md) documents the existing playable prototype and its live iteration controls; it does not establish the finished visual standard.
