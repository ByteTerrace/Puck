- [x] The Moth turns and moves directionally through authored rig data ([how the moth moves](../../.claude/skills/sdf-authoring/references/rig.md#how-the-moth-moves)). A forward diagonal turns her hips and legs about 40 degrees into the travel direction while her chest turns most of the way back and her head keeps the facing; a strafe steps sideways and a backpedal strides backward, because each thigh strides in the plane of travel (`travelDirection`). Her hips trail a turn and settle with a small overshoot while her chest and head lead it (`turnRate` through a `dynamics` row). A curving run leans her into the turn, a start leans her forward and a stop back (`lateralAcceleration`, `forwardAcceleration`). A resting foot holds its spot and steps once it drifts 9 cm, one foot at a time (`plant.replant`), so turning in place and settling after a stop step rather than slide. All of it is presentation only.
