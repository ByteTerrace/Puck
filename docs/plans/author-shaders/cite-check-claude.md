# Citation check of the author-shader research

Checked in the research round. Findings only; the two research files and all tracked files are unchanged. No build, commit, merge, or push was performed.

CONFIRMED means the source actually says the claim, not that its measurements generalize to Puck or were independently reproduced. PARTLY identifies a narrower statement or a material qualification. NOT FOUND means the cited page does not supply the claim; **uncited** identifies an inference with no supporting citation. UNREACHABLE means the content could not be opened. Multiple assertions share a row only where they share the same verdict. Repeated claims in summaries inherit the identified row's verdict.

I opened every explicit source URL. Some web-tool failures were recovered by directly downloading the same URL; these are not classified UNREACHABLE. The 34 MB Dreams PDF was downloaded and extracted with pypdf (145 PDF pages). Dolphin archive links failed, but the corresponding original articles opened; their content verdicts below use those originals. Section names and source headings locate evidence without relying on a search snippet. Reference labels below link directly to the pages checked.

## Verdicts that change a conclusion

- Rejecting raw HLSL is not established by the platform-policy, costing, or spelling-equivalence citations (E44/E70, S22–36/S69–72/S97).
- Static work counts do not prove frame-time/TDR safety; the relight precedent admits oversized units, and DRED attribution is qualified (S05/S37–46/S56–57).
- Document-known materials do not prove a complete, small PSO set or immediate, equivalent Puck interpreter output (E50/E73–77, S91–96).
- Dolphin supports hybrid as a candidate, not a proven Puck requirement; its Deck result is game-specific, and default versus popularity is no disagreement (S89–95).
- Dreams supports parameter-based expression, not a shipped static-time budget; the figures are prototypes and include arithmetic errors (S08–20).
- Custom lighting is not universally forward-only: Unity allows deferred customization through shader code (E05/E34).
- Substrate cost, Godot compositor caching, Slang material restrictions, and the SM7 roadmap all need narrower conclusions (E14/E49, S59/S63/S66–67).
