## Answers to the constraint review

| Finding | Revision and check |
|---|---|
| H1. Forearm clearance and OFF state | A transverse cut through the blower shows the open forearm bore, with both the blower and duct contained in the outer wall. The exterior views establish the squat proportions and diagonal amber placement. The README identifies the cyan ring in OFF as the module power light. |
| H2. Four-metre playing pose | The chase view shows the tucked playing pose from behind the left shoulder. The arms stay in front of the chest, with the right hand at the deck as the front view shows. The torso and arm hide the vents from this angle. The README records the result for the built chase camera check. |
| H3. Turnaround and profile caption | FRONT shows the forearms across the chest. LEFT PROFILE looks along the same forearms, foreshortening them against the chest. The left side of the suit is nearest the camera and she faces image left. |
| H4. Playing arm during locomotion | Both strips now show the Moth travelling image left from the front left quarter. In every frame the visible left arm leads through the shell and cyan ring to the free left glove. The right arm crosses to the deck. |
| H5. Wrist ring position | The cyan ring sits at the glove opening in FRONT, LEFT PROFILE and all four transition beats. The left gauntlet runs from louvres through amber meter and four vents to cyan at the wrist. Rear-view occlusion hides the ring where the torso and upper arm cover it. |
| H6. Pilot fit and orientation | Both pilot views put the louvred intake at the elbow, then the amber meter and deck, with cyan at the wrist. The pilot wears a smaller version of the design over her sleeve, shown at her scale. |
