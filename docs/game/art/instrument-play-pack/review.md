## Revisions selected

The instrument, playing stance, fingering study, dance stance and stage
distance use their second revisions. The airflow cutaway now joins the
blower to the manifold and outlet. The chase treatment puts light on the
actual vent deck. The fingering sheet uses an unpowered reach study to
show contact clearly, and the stance diagram fixes the support mark and
the order of the footprints. The stage revisions remove misplaced vent
decks. The first revision of the bare-hand sheet gives the touching finger
one small bore and keeps a single breath meter. The walking and running
strips, signature moves and transition use their initial images.

The closer sheets are the reference for fingertip contact and cuff
construction. Read the chase and crowd sheets for silhouette and light
placement. The planned implementation checks judge the same poses from
the default chase boom, side and hand cameras, and then from the valley
stage and the gallery call-and-response exhibit.
