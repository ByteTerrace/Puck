# Moth instrument and dance generation prompts

Codex used the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool with one opaque PNG per
call. Reference images supplied character, prop or environment continuity.
The prompts below are the submitted text, grouped by panel. See the
[written audit](review.md) for selection and review status.

## 1. Instrument

[Moth construction details](../moth-concept-pack/02-construction-details.png) supplied the gauntlet mass and its outer vents.

```text
Landscape 3:2 professional videogame concept development sheet with original slightly futuristic product design, stylized painterly toon shading on large clear forms, colored cool shadows, warm cream linen paper ground, restrained dusty lavender / warm ivory shells and charcoal pivots, little ochre, tiny functional cyan light. Puck Canyon Workshop art direction. No brand or borrowed real/game designs. Clean thin rules and spacious layout.
The object is an original forearm-mounted wind instrument that looks like a compact stepped rectangular armor block with rounded corners. It's built flush into the Moth's existing blocky LEFT gauntlet, ivory outside with dusty lavender inner side. Two charcoal slot recesses of the original outer face are reused as a pair of two-position vent banks. Each bank is bisected by one ivory rib, resulting in exactly four oblong dark finger-stop apertures in a stable compact 2-by-2 layout on a beveled wristward outer corner accessible from the opposite hand, with a very thin separately lit cyan rim visible from left rear three quarters. Plate screws are forbidden. A thumb-width low-profile graduated ochre breath bar is set into the neighboring sloped face, with three narrow pale amber bands. One small baffled air intake at the elbowward end, one internal microblower supplying a sealed folded resonant air channel, and one dark forward-facing broad slit below the vent shelf for the acoustic outlet, distinct from the four finger vents. No mouthpiece, tube, hoses, external straps, handles or weapon cues. The wrist collar is open to the glove and there is zero palm obstruction.
Panel 1, INSTRUMENT. Design the object in the reference gauntlet's massing and new function, do not draw people. From left to right across the upper row draw three aligned matching product turnaround views on one base line: an OUTER FACE orthographic showing two rows of two vents, a narrow WRIST END view showing the unobstructed dark wrist collar and the shallow lip along one outer edge, and a REAR 3/4 view showing the same beveled panel. In the lower row draw one large unambiguous SIDE SECTION cutaway with a single continuous teal airflow arrow from elbow intake through the blower and folded duct to forward acoustic outlet, also connecting branches to the vent deck. Beside it show two smaller identical rear 3/4 crops under matching illumination OFF and ACTIVE. OFF vent rims are charcoal, breath bar dark. ACTIVE exactly four discrete cyan vent rims and a three-segment amber breath meter. Render the main views with real convincing plate thickness and room for an armored wrist. Use only the short requested labels, no legends, no extra parts.
```
