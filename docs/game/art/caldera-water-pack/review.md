# Caldera water visual audit

The artist inspected all seven generated candidates and the six exported
keepers. Under this assignment's explicit owner ruling, the primary artist
is also the deciding judge. No independent checking-partner approval is
claimed. This is the scoped review arrangement for this pack under
[concept art review](../README.md#concept-art-review).

## Audit criteria

All seven headings from the art policy were checked. For these environment
panels, attachment and continuity include grounded ice, connected water,
banks, stairs, rails and architectural supports. Physical coherence includes
downhill inflow, horizontal free surfaces, impact location and the float's
contact with water. Camera perspective includes foreshortening of the lake,
consistent depth and the matched before/after view. Compositing checks include
ice/rock, water/bank and wet/dry contacts and edit boundaries. Agreement with
the turnaround becomes agreement with selected B, the established landscape
and repeated landmarks. There are no posed characters or character turnarounds:
anatomical joint range, thrust, weight over feet and character fixed facts
are not applicable. Fixed environment facts are audited explicitly below.

## Builder and export checks

The [handoff](README.md) retains the composition map's north-west falls,
centre-west river, pool west of spawn, bridge route and south-west overlook.
The close-up rail and pool-side landing are local inspection features. The
reservoir cutaway communicates an instrument built into the place; it does
not relocate the existing overlook or expose the entire cave in play.

The time-course table distinguishes generated states from the unpictured
filling/full transition. Glacier disappearance at three hours, retained lake,
dry falls and settled river are explicit. The gauge reads actual reservoir
level; the feature lane must drive it from that state. Perspective paintings
are not dimensional or hydraulic measurements.

Final reduced exports preserve material distinctions, spray, submerged
stones and float contact at delivery size. The before/after files share
1280 × 548 dimensions and matched framing. The taller gauge section is 1024
pixels wide. Each kept PNG is below 2,000,000 bytes. The artist accepts all
six for their stated builder roles. No engine build, World boot, simulation
verification or independent partner review forms part of this art audit.
