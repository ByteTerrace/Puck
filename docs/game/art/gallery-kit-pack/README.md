# Gallery kit for the modeller

Build one exhibit station beside a 1.75 m visitor, then use it to set the scale of the aisle, windows and balcony around it. These six component sheets and the level-zero hall painting develop the owner's selected [Canyon workshop](../valley-and-gallery-concepts/canyon-workshop.png).

## Read in this order

| Image | What to model |
|---|---|
| [Display plinth](display-plinth.png) | Set the body about 1.8 m wide and the cap 0.75 m above the floor. The front view places the controls within reach. The side view gives the lectern slope and overhang. The three-quarter view shows how much of the cap the turntable occupies. |
| [Branched pier and arcade](branched-pier-and-arcade.png) | Block out the two openings between three shafts. Trace one unbroken curve from the shaft into the underside of the slab. The side section gives the bearing depth. The close view places the brass strips on the central face. |
| [Window bay](window-bay.png) | Seat the glass in the teal frame inside the cream surround. Leave space outside the mullion for the orange leaves to spread and stack. The section at right shows the hub bracket and both glazing pockets. |
| [Balcony edge and stair rail](balcony-and-stair-rail.png) | Start at the post beside the top tread. Carry the brass rail through the bend, cut the glass panels to meet at the post, then follow the section down to the fixing behind the cream fascia. |
| [Furniture and planting](furniture-and-planting.png) | Use the knee-height seat to place the bench and planter. Group the foliage into a spiky crown, broad leaves and low growth. The rod brackets and enlarged frame profile show how the wall pieces stand proud of the wall. |
| [Floor and ceiling surfaces](floor-and-ceiling.png) | Set one square terracotta tile to 1.2 m on a side. The grout marks that pitch across the sheet. Carry the joints across the flush cream pad. Above it, use the shadow inside each coffer to distinguish its rim, back and round light. |
| [Level-zero exhibit hall](level-zero-exhibit-hall.png) | Judge the assembled room from the centre aisle with the camera 1.6 m above the floor. The near controls, the exhibits beyond them and the canyon beyond the windows should all be readable from that spot. |

Set dimensions in the blockout. The figures show reach and clearance, and the enlarged details show how parts meet. In the hall, leave space to stand at a console while another visitor passes behind. The nearest exhibits frame the aisle and the far window gives it a destination.

The existing packs supply the [building and circulation](../canyon-workshop-pack/README.md) and the [workshop activities](../canyon-discovery-pack/README.md). For this level-zero target, use the [gallery kit studies](../gallery-kit-studies.md) alongside the sheets.
