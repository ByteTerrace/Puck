# Canyon workshop visual audit

The artist (the generating Codex session) and the checking partner (the
independent `canyon_art_audit` agent) inspected every delivered panel against
the [selected B baseline](../valley-and-gallery-concepts/canyon-workshop.png)
and the [concept art review criteria](../README.md#concept-art-review).

The environment checks cover visible attachment and continuity, support and
assembly, scale cues, agreement with the selected design, camera perspective,
and compositing seams. These sheets contain no Moth character study or
motion sequence; they make no claim about an unseen joint's range or a
mechanism's runtime behavior.

## Corrections before handoff

The initial atrium repeated occupied lift capsules vertically in the shaft.
It was rejected and edited to show one car at ground level with empty travel
space above. Both partners accepted the corrected image.

The initial alternate exterior showed the feature facade with reversed wing
handedness and did not establish a rear view. It was replaced with the
rear/service-side view, with the front tower mostly occluded and service access
visible. Both partners accepted the replacement.

Those initial drafts are excluded from the delivered pack. All six keeper
images, comprising eight panels, pass the visual review for what they depict.
Consistent blockout dimensions and unseen connections are developed by the
modeller using the [handoff](README.md#modeller-handoff).
