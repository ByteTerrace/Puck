# Round-two companion prompts

The seven panels were generated with Codex's built-in ImageGen tool. The
scene prompts describe the settings in words and use drawings of the two
characters as references. The scale sheet and the two scenes were revised
with the built-in editor. [The design and panel audit](README.md#round-two)

## Exploration

A new four-concept sheet.

```text

Create one polished landscape 2x2 exploration sheet for Puck, hard equal quadrants, warm neutral sketchbook background, consistent painterly game prop rendering, bold toy-like silhouettes, dry gouache, simple value masses, no typography or labels or humans. Each cell has ONE clearly distinct palm-size flying companion, each seen in an informative three-quarter view, all with strong silhouette even without lights. TOP LEFT: Knotwhistle, a creature-instrument, shaped like a solid asymmetric wooden branch fork carried lengthwise, long lower prong and short raised upper prong with an unmistakable empty open-ended horizontal gap between them (two prongs growing from ONE joined thick heel). One broad deep-teal folded paper sail canted upward from the heel on a single oversized solid brass pivot, with a truncated point like a folded leaf. Honey-dark walnut and russet wood prongs with blunt ends, exactly one brass shoe around the tip of the longer lower prong. A narrow warm ivory line of bioluminescence is recessed into the INSIDE of the lower branch in the open fork, like the seam of a speaking reed; no glowing eyes. Aim for a striking top-heavy teal flag and dark sideways open fork profile. TOP RIGHT: Spore Choir, a loose vertical helix of FIVE independently flying quince-colored spore flakes with broad flat ragged edges, no central body, a stack of staggered almond slivers like five notes on a curved invisible staff; mint tips light locally. BOTTOM LEFT: Lichen Kite, a GROWN living lichen ramet, a wide planar sage-green three-lobed ruffled fan with asymmetric scalloped edge, bent vine stem and root tassel, short bars of chartreuse dotted along branching veins, nothing manufactured. BOTTOM RIGHT: Foldbell, a hand-built clockwork pop-up wayfinding artifact, a stout burnt-orange timber rectangular stamp with a single hinged ochre accordion paper pennant opened out vertically above it, simple brass block hinges and a little crescent under its rectangular base. Four different ontologies and entirely different silhouettes. Draw tangible coherent attachments on the manufactured parts. All forms intact; no faces, screens, eyeballs, balls, robots, propellers, spaceships, four-prong cubes, white pods, glow haze or floating particles; Spore Choir bodies are the only designed disconnected pieces. Original game artist ideation sheet, spacious high quality shapes and paper surface.
```
