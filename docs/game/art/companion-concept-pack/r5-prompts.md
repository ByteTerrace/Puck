## States and distance samples

The states panel is a drawn front-elevation schematic. It uses one set of
paths for the body and its two attached wing plates. Each state's held pose
sets the wing and head angles. Filled shapes show its lantern mask and wing
accents on a flat dark ground, without a simulated bloom or running light.

The projected neutral height in pixels is the image height times the body
height, divided by twice the distance times the tangent of half the vertical
field of view. At 1080 pixels, 60° vertical field of view and 0.16 m body
height, that gives 29.9 pixels at 5 m and 10.0 pixels at 15 m. Each pose
uses the same scale so banks and dips keep their actual projected extents.
The greyscale panels use 0.2126 red, 0.7152 green and 0.0722 blue on the same
pose. View the PNG at 100% to see the samples at their rendered sizes.
