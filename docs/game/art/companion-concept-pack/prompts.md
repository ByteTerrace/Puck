# Companion concept prompts

The six panels were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The prompts
below produced the retained images.

## Turnaround

Generated from the written brief.

> Create a polished landscape original game-character turnaround on warm off-white paper, four equally sized, separate aligned views in a single horizontal row, from left to right FRONT, RIGHT SIDE, BACK, FRONT THREE-QUARTER (small clean labels below, no other writing). One same exact toy-like companion in each view. Character: a palm-sized hovering mechanical seed lantern named Pip, a squat broad upside-down teardrop seed with a creamy warm ivory ceramic hood, a softly tapered dark deep-teal keel, and two chunky matte ochre seed-shaped lateral fins attached to two round dark teal hinge discs halfway down its sides. Rounded finite parts, nothing floating. Two fins sweep down and rear, hugging the body, with broad silhouettes that protrude beyond both sides, one fin per hinge. Its front has a single wide recessed opaque dark teal oval lens, blank except for a single narrow warm-white smile-shaped line low in the lens (light aperture, not teeth; no eyes, LEDs, or markings above it). The back has an ivory hood wrapping continuously around to the tapered rear keel, two small horizontal dark vents on the keel, and no face. Single dark nozzle flush under the keel, no exhaust for these views. No antenna, weapons, rotors, glazing bubbles, floating parts, extra arms, wings, ears, calves, feet or legs. Establish the exact same joint placement, colors, contours and orientation cues in all views. Identity should be charming like a little curious seed-pod, chunky and handmade with a sophisticated painterly toon game look, restrained broad tone facets and dry brush edges, subtle material wear, no photoreal metal. Orthographic drawings with neutral light. Entire body in frame and high enough detail to build in simple rounded SDF volumes. Create the image.

## With the Moth

References were the companion turnaround and the Moth’s suited turnaround.

> Create a landscape original game concept illustration on warm off-white paper. First reference is the seed lantern turnaround, second is the Moth's suited turnaround. Draw just one full-body Moth in a relaxed planted front three-quarter stance, and one seed lantern hovering over her OPEN relaxed left gauntlet, with clear air between. Both at the same distance from the camera. The same tiny hovering seed lantern as the turnaround reference: pointed ivory ceramic hood, recessed dark teal oval face, dark teal tapering keel with one bottom nozzle, two broad ochre seed fins hinged to the sides by teal discs. The blank dark lens has only one simple luminous pattern and no eyes. Retain the painterly toon treatment and the exact hardware. This is a scale plate: the Moth's suited height is 2.06 m, the lantern's height is 0.24 m EXACTLY about 12 percent of the figure from boots to hood in the drawing. It should appear slightly narrower than her gauntlet. Use a dark rim and one thin warm-white curved smile on the tiny companion. Preserve the Moth's adult brown face, amber eyes, one dark braid under the front of her ivory lined lavender hood, chunky lavender and ivory armor, charcoal joints and soles, no earrings, full five-digit gauntlets. Boots level on the same neutral contact plane, no measuring graphics or labels, no other props, no copies, no seams. Readable silhouettes, game production concept art.

## Light language

Reference was the companion turnaround.

> Create a precise landscape game concept LIGHT LANGUAGE sheet, warm neutral paper design, a spaced 4 columns by 2 rows grid. Draw EXACTLY eight identical small front views, fixed geometry and angles. The same tiny hovering seed lantern as the turnaround reference: pointed ivory ceramic hood, recessed dark teal oval face, dark teal tapering keel with one bottom nozzle, two broad ochre seed fins hinged to the sides by teal discs. The blank dark lens has only one simple luminous pattern and no eyes. Retain the painterly toon treatment and the exact hardware. Each face shows a single bold, geometric luminous glyph on blank dark teal. Glyphs stay behind the ceramic lip, no exterior graphic or glow haze. Top row left to right: label "IDLE" thin ivory U-shaped smile; "INTEREST" cyan horizontal pill dash; "CURIOSITY" lilac vertical pill dash; "DISCOVERY" mint outline circle. Bottom row left to right: "OBJECTIVE" warm golden outline diamond; "DANGER" orange three-sided outline triangle pointing UP, empty interior; "LOCK" icy blue four right-angle corner brackets forming four corners of a missing square, empty interior; "LOW ENERGY" amber single tiny dot near bottom of lens. Use these exact eight English labels each centered BELOW its character. Do not invent any other text, eyes, marks, boxes, diagrams, or UI. All glyphs legible. Keep geometric design distinct even in black and white.

## Speech aperture

Reference was the companion turnaround.

> Create a landscape game-character speech aperture concept sheet, warm off-white paper. Four evenly spaced same-size front views of one same toy-like seed lantern The same tiny hovering seed lantern as the turnaround reference: pointed ivory ceramic hood, recessed dark teal oval face, dark teal tapering keel with one bottom nozzle, two broad ochre seed fins hinged to the sides by teal discs. The blank dark lens has only one simple luminous pattern and no eyes. Retain the painterly toon treatment and the exact hardware. Show one smooth cycle of a warm ivory light aperture low on the dark lens. From left to right, exactly these four frames: REST (a thin shallow smile curve, dim), ONSET (short thin horizontal glowing oval, slightly open), VOWEL (same central shape expands to bold tall glowing oval), RELEASE (short thin horizontal glowing oval again, dimmer). All centers at the same height on the dark glass, with untouched dark area above; no eyes or mechanical jaw. Keep pose, wings, shape and camera absolutely identical across frames. One exact small simple English label below each drawing, REST, ONSET, VOWEL, RELEASE, with no other text. Character design model sheet with warm soft neutral light, no haze. Each silhouette fits fully into its column. This is intended to communicate mouth movement through light only.

## Gallery discovery

Reference was the companion turnaround. The buildings and setting came from the written direction.

> Paint an original landscape (16:9) game keyframe at standing player eye level inside a bright painterly toon museum whose massive living tree threads pale ceramic and warm wood balconies with deep teal ribs, planted rails and giant canyon windows. CLEARLY FIRST-PERSON CAMERA: no foreground human, no silhouettes, no people, no visible body parts. The interaction is centered on a waist-high octagonal cream-and-ochre exhibit plinth, about ONE METRE WIDE; include all of its width. A teal geometric mineral sculpture stands on it. At its near left corner, paint the tiny identical mechanical seed companion from the reference hovering with a visible gap about one body-height over the top. It is exactly 24 cm tall and appears about ONE QUARTER of the pedestal's apparent width, about 120–180 pixels tall in a 1600px painting. Companion seen modest front three-quarter, its pointed ivory ceramic hood, its teal bottom keel with dark nozzle and two ochre rounded fins fastened to dark round side hinges, teal lens showing a mint OUTLINE CIRCLE. Paint a faint narrow mint beam STRAIGHT DOWN from the nozzle, exactly along the lens character's vertical body axis to a tight mint ring that lies on the plinth top directly below. The ring must lie flat with believable projection and not straddle the plinth edge. This effect is gentle and local; it does not light the room. Keep clear contact shadows, grounded exhibits, quiet framing, no words or graphics. The companion is a small physical object within the finished painting, not an insert close to the lens. Use the input as industrial design reference only.

## Valley danger

Reference was the companion turnaround. The cliffs and path came from the written direction.

> Paint a landscape (16:9) original stylized game keyframe, eye height, deep caldera at blue hour. Preserve a sense of scale: a single tiny 24 cm hovering companion in the near foreground left of center, only about one fifth of picture height, in clear air. A patch of waist-high vegetation on the near bank gives it real small scale, and a broad walkable path is completely unobstructed to its right. Use the supplied turnaround as the exact model for the miniature character: pointed ivory ceramic hood, ochre seed fins attached to teal hinges, teal tapered keel with one nozzle, dark teal oval lens with only one unmistakable warm orange outline triangle with empty interior. In front three-quarter, it has just pulled back and rolled slightly toward the viewer as if to stop them. No beam, no aura, no exhaust, no words, no UI, no people. Just beyond the bend show an obvious active localized rockfall with fragments and small natural dust at the foot of a steep ochre shelf, safely separated from the hovering companion. The path leads the eye to the debris. Background features warmly layered cliffs, blue-green trees, two modest distinctly cratered moons and distant warm gallery windows, quiet and airy, no clipping to black. High-quality original painterly toon adventure art, graphic areas of value and color, directional warm edge light and blue shadows. The foreground figure must be fully integrated into the scene with atmospheric light, not pasted or enlarged.
