# Round-four companion prompts

Codex used built-in ImageGen for the concept paintings. The prompts below
record the plates used in the pack. The Puck scenes were described in words
using the Canyon workshop direction. [Round four](README.md#round-four) ·
[Moth turnaround](../moth-concept-pack/01-suited-turnaround.png) ·
[Canyon workshop direction](../valley-and-gallery.md#selected-art-treatment)

The scale sheet crops the two figures with their contact shadows. The source
figures measure roughly 666 pixels for the Moth and 253 for Spur. Resizing the
two crops to 559 and 130 pixels puts them at the same scale. The states
sheet crops each large pose, matches the size of the head housing across
sources, and downsamples each pose twice to the same distance. The greyscale
copy uses luma weights of 0.2126 red, 0.7152 green and 0.0722 blue. The 30 m
profile comes from the solid Spur thumbnail on the exploration sheet.

## Shared character reference

The prompts marked `[Spur design]` include the following text.

```text
Use ONLY THE SECOND CHARACTER FROM LEFT of the four-character reference, the tiny left-facing
kingfisher mech, as exact design reference. Call it Spur. The signature outline consists of a
lifted helmet brow with a short rectangular ochre single-lens camera cassette jutting forward at
eye height, two little brass forks underneath, a visible brass neck ring, one chest-heavy deep
teal thorax with a cream breast, two short broad folded cream wing plates with teal moth-teardrop
markings and round brass wing pivots, exactly two jointed dark gripping feet with brass pivots,
and a striking asymmetrical tail: only ONE tall flat teal left blade that bends upwards behind the
head height and has an ivory tip; the right tail feather is a short ochre stub at the base. Body
reads as a bird. No extra eyes, fins, cables, antennae, guns, biological feathers, rotors, or
floating plates. Stylized painterly toy-tech, deep teal, ochre enamel and ivory with worn
chamfers, restrained brass and charcoal.
```

