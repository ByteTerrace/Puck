# Valley and gallery visual references

The valley and gallery's concept art starts with real landscapes and architecture,
translated into Puck's own stylized forms. This page collects candidate references
and explains what each can contribute. The owner selected study B, Canyon
workshop, as the baseline for the next concept wave and modeller handoff.

## Owner direction

Use real-life sources purely as references for the stylized game. The setting is
slightly futuristic and belongs with the Moth's power suit. The treatment is
painterly, with The Wind Waker and quality toon animation as examples of the
desired visual character.

The images must read as cartoony and futuristic; the real-life references supply
ingredients for that treatment. Study B is the selected baseline: warm layered
canyon cliffs, cream and ochre modules, deep teal structure, rounded rectangular
openings, orange pivoting sunshades, and the exposed glass lift tower. Subsequent
views develop that design rather than reopening the four-way direction choice.

The [Canyon workshop environment pack](canyon-workshop-pack/README.md) develops
the selected B into varied views, room scenes and construction details for the
modeller session.

The [Canyon discovery gallery](canyon-discovery-pack/README.md) develops the
major demos into connected rooms and walk-up play/learning activities.

The existing [valley brief](../../plans/valley-and-moons.md#the-owners-rulings)
places the gallery on the clear floor of a deep basin or caldera, with close
slopes, an overlook, and two cratered moons, one lighter and one darker. The
[gallery](../../../worlds/debug/shapes.md) has four storeys, exhibits on plinths,
a painting wing, stairs, a lift, and a board game room. References inform a
redesign without silently replacing those functions.

## Candidate references

| Real place | Reference qualities | Proposed translation for Puck |
|---|---|---|
| [Sete Cidades, Azores](https://volcano.si.edu/volcano.cfm?vn=382080) | A volcanic caldera enclosing lakes; its rim gives a strong enclosing landscape silhouette. | Use the enclosing bowl and green slopes to organize the valley around its clear, usable floor. Paint large rock and vegetation masses; keep the floor's space rather than reproducing the lakes. |
| [Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland](https://www.myswitzerland.com/en-gb/destinations/lauterbrunnen/) | Steep valley walls and waterfalls make vertical scale immediately legible. | Explore a few distinct cliff faces and a slender waterfall as flight landmarks. Compress geological detail into broad painted planes and ledges. |
| [Teshima Art Museum, Japan](https://benesse-artsite.jp/en/art/teshima-artmuseum.html) | A low, continuous concrete shell with two oval openings that admit light, wind, and sound. | Explore a curved entrance canopy, rounded apertures, and a generous lobby. Adapt the shell vocabulary to the gallery's multiple floors rather than adopting the museum's single low room. |
| [Chichu Art Museum, Naoshima, Japan](https://benesse-artsite.jp/en/art/chichu.html) | Mostly underground architecture integrated with its landscape, with natural light changing the rooms through the day. | Explore framed valley views, geometric light wells, sheltered courts, and transitions between intimate passages and open exhibition rooms. Give exhibits controlled lighting and clear silhouettes. |
| [Gardens by the Bay, Singapore](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/plan-your-visit/itinerary-planner/SDW-GBTB-design-trail.html) | The Supertrees combine planting with engineered structures; some incorporate solar collection or ventilation functions. | Explore useful planted infrastructure: shade canopies, an overlook, and visible environmental equipment. Simplify it into a few large, readable structures. |

These are references for spatial relationships, silhouettes, light, and the
relationship between manufactured objects and nature. Their buildings, artworks,
and layouts are not Puck assets or blueprints to reproduce.

## Selected art treatment

Study B pairs warm stratified canyon walls with a chunky, stepped museum of
cream and ochre modules over deep teal arches. The enclosing caldera and green
floor remain. Lauterbrunnen supplies a study of vertical scale; the Japanese
museums supply studies of light and space; Singapore supplies a study of
functioning technology among plants. The owner selected B as the baseline for
the next wave, not an exact measured building plan.

Keep foreground, middle distance, and rim distinct in value and colour. Build
cliffs from large planes, vegetation from grouped shapes, and clouds from clear
painted silhouettes. Use restrained brush variation within those masses,
coloured shadows, and selective sharp edges around paths, doors, and exhibits.
The painterly treatment needs to hold from the player's moving camera.

The futuristic vocabulary can relate to the
[Moth's suit](moth-concept-pack/README.md#preserve-the-character): thick shell
panels over darker recesses, visible pivots where something moves, inset glazing,
and small functional lights. Explore pale mineral surfaces, warm interior
materials, and selective colour accents as a building palette. The suit's own
lavender and ivory remain character facts, not a requirement that every building
wear the same colours.

## Concept studies

The first generated comparisons pair an exterior and an interior on each sheet.
They explore design language and mood, not dimensioned construction. B is the
selected baseline; A, C and D remain comparison studies. The
[prompt set](valley-and-gallery-concepts/prompts.md) records the
generation instructions and tool provenance.

| Study | Exploration | Image |
|---|---|---|
| A: Garden shell | Green caldera, rounded ivory shells, warm open atrium. | [Green shell](valley-and-gallery-concepts/green-shell.png) |
| B: Canyon workshop | Warm stratified cliffs, ochre modules, exposed structural arches and lift. | [Canyon workshop](valley-and-gallery-concepts/canyon-workshop.png) |
| C: Valley lantern | Blue-hour basin, amber-lit circular gallery, articulated roof shades. | [Twilight lantern](valley-and-gallery-concepts/twilight-lantern.png) |
| D: Bold cartoon futurism | Simpler colour planes, exaggerated shells and visible machinery. | [Bold cartoon](valley-and-gallery-concepts/bold-cartoon.png) |

Start with a valley view from human height that shows the close walls, clear
floor, gallery silhouette, and a route to the overlook. Follow with a gallery
arrival view that shows the entrance, public circulation, and its connection to
the landscape. An interior study should show an exhibit room and a connection
between floors, keeping the plinths and portal paintings readable.

These are exploratory compositions. Before a sheet becomes an implementation
reference, review its scale, structure, circulation, lighting, and consistency
with the [Moth reference pack](moth-concept-pack/README.md) and the
[concept art review](README.md#concept-art-review).
