# Valley and gallery concept prompts

## D: Bold cartoon futurism

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Generate an original CARTOON FUTURISTIC game environment concept sheet for Puck. ONE wide landscape image, two-panel diptych: left two thirds exterior valley and gallery, right third interior of the same building. No text, no labels, no lettering.
THIS STUDY MUST BE VERY CARTOONY. Think confidently drawn feature-animation background and stylized cel-shaded adventure game, Wind Waker-level graphic simplicity. Oversized curved silhouettes, bold dark blue contour accents, large FLAT colour areas, only TWO shadow tones per surface, a few visible gouache strokes. Graphic spiral cloud shapes, trees in bold round clumps, simple gigantic geometric rock planes. Render every architectural surface as a painted cartoon, with flat matte colour and hard simple shadows. Avoid detailed realistic architecture, photoreal materials, cinematic realism, glossy marble, high-frequency foliage, painterly realism or realistic 3D rendering. It should immediately look like a frame from a charming FUTURISTIC CARTOON.
Setting: lush deep enclosed volcanic caldera, ring of close 350-metre-high cliffs, clear grassy floor about 400 metres across, vivid turquoise waterfall off a broad cliff ledge, one rim overlook. Sky cobalt blue with stylized creamy clouds, two modest cratered moons, one pale one darker. Reference real volcanic landscapes only for the enclosing landform; radically simplify.
Gallery: build an instantly recognizable chunky RETRO-FUTURE public museum about four storeys tall and much smaller than cliffs. Four stacked off-centre rounded disk-like exhibition halls around a big visible central elevator mast. Thick creamy armour-like outer shells over dark blue mechanical joints, broad dusty-lavender and warm orange cladding accents, big turquoise oval windows, a few oversized circular hinges and piston-supported moveable sunshades, rounded portal-like entry arch with modest cyan edge light. Mix stepped cylinders and chamfered blocks into a bold coherent silhouette. Real supports connect every projection; museum not flying, not spaceship, not castle. Give it one huge welcoming entrance and an uncluttered curving path across the open meadow. Architecture feels related to chunky powered armour: strong plates, a few readable moving mechanisms, no dense greebles. A few extremely simplified tiny visitors for scale. No aircraft.
Interior: same chunky cream/lavender/navy/orange language, a large cylindrical glass lift in a dark support mast, three upper curved balconies and a broad stair, two or three oversized simple geometric art exhibits on round plinths, framed colourful portal paintings. Big oval window looks into green valley. Warm ochre floor painted FLAT, no mirror reflection. Light and shadow as clean painted shapes. Strong useful cartoon game readability. This is the bold simple playful-futurism option, not a realistic museum painting.
```

These studies explore the owner's cartoony, futuristic, painterly direction.
They are alternatives for discussion, not approved implementation references.
Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each sheet pairs an exterior
and an interior of the same proposed gallery.

Real-life reference sources and existing world requirements are collected in
[Valley and gallery visual references](../valley-and-gallery.md).

## A · Green caldera / shell gallery

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: exploratory environment concept art for Puck, a cartoony futuristic game. Produce ONE landscape concept sheet as a polished TWO-PANEL DIPTYCH: left panel occupies about 65% of width and shows the valley and gallery exterior from a low overlook near ground level, right panel occupies about 35% and shows a coherent interior of that SAME gallery. One narrow simple divider; no labels, no captions, no typography. Both panels are fully painted scenes, not architectural diagrams.
Visual style is unmistakably CARTOON FUTURISM: confident exaggerated rounded shapes, carefully designed silhouettes, strong graphic composition, crisp 2-3 tone cel-shaded light and shadow with selective gouache brush texture, richly coloured bounce light, stylized painted cloud shapes, grouped foliage, broad readable rock planes. High-quality hand-painted feature animation / Wind Waker-like environmental clarity and charm. Prioritize shape and colour; NOT photorealism, NOT realistic archviz, NOT gritty sci-fi, NOT generic detailed fantasy illustration, NOT blurry impressionism. Futurism must be immediately visible in manufactured forms, inset glazing, chunky mechanical joints, integrated lighting and clever circulation. Friendly, adventurous, functional.
World requirements: a compact deep caldera with close steep walls rising about 300-430 metres around a clear roughly 400-metre-wide usable floor. The gallery is an approximately four-storey/18-metre-tall public museum, a landmark on the floor, dwarfed by the enclosing terrain. Keep open walkable ground, clear paths, a visible entrance, and an overlook in the rim. Two distinct cratered moons, one pale and one darker, visible unobtrusively in the sky; no rings. Interior has generous circulation, an evident stair and lift connection to upper levels, a handful of bold abstract geometric sculptural exhibits on plinths and framed luminous portal paintings. No vehicles or aircraft. Tiny visitors can convey scale but no foreground character portrait. Architecture must feel inhabitable and structurally coherent. This is an original synthesized game environment, never a copy of an existing museum.

Direction A: THE GARDEN SHELL. Reference qualities: lush enclosing volcanic terrain of the Azores; continuous sculptural roof surfaces and sky apertures of Teshima, adapted into ORIGINAL multi-storey cartoon museum architecture. Bright late-morning sunshine. Saturated spring greens and teal leafy masses, exposed warm grey rock ledges, one elegant narrow waterfall, distant planes lavender-blue. The gallery is a welcoming stack of three or four thick ivory rounded shell volumes wrapping an open glazed central atrium. Chunky dark recessed joints and turquoise glass between shells, sparse ochre wayfinding colour, restrained cyan operational lights. Show a readable broad entrance canopy and gently curved paved approach amid grass; no city. The central floor stays mostly dry open land, not a lake. Give the shell rims a beautifully exaggerated thick profile and a few visible pivoting sunshades, making the technology explicit.
Interior right panel: warm ivory curved balconies visibly stacked around an airy atrium, a thick exposed lift capsule guided by a dark structural mast, broad curved stairs, soft mint and apricot colour accents, large oval sky opening, sculptural exhibits clearly separated on dark rounded plinths, a framed portal painting as a luminous square of garden colour. Dramatic but simple painted shadow shapes; clear cartoon edges. Ground camera at visitor height.
```
