# Canyon discovery gallery review

The artist inspected each delivered panel for visible attachment, support,
perspective, scale, compositing seams and agreement with B. Independent checking
partners inspected the same panels against their intended demonstration.
The [concept art review](../README.md#concept-art-review) applies to this pack.

The `canyon_art_audit` partner checked the overview, shapes and portals.
The `device_demo_inventory` partner checked hardware, cables and Wonderland
scale. The `demo_layout_inventory` partner checked the games, studio/trail and
source accuracy of the handoff. All were read-only reviews; the artist generated
the corrections and both sides inspected the delivered keepers.

## Corrections made

The initial commons had twelve stations. It was replaced with a high view of
four clusters of four. The subtraction panel's solid cutter was removed. The
portal pair's outdoor views were replaced with the indoor concourse, and the
fall views repeat that same room. The overview's repeated lift capsules and
platform across the fall line were removed. Wonderland's oversized handheld
and rest were reduced, and its television content was replaced with a test
card. The rejected drafts are excluded from this pack.

The final eight images comprise eleven panels. Both the artist and the relevant
checking partner accept all eleven for the roles above. Exact dimensions,
board topology, device internals and unseen connections remain governed by the
source documents and the modeller's coherent blockout, rather than incidental
details in perspective paintings.

## Source accuracy

The handoff preserves every major demo family identified in the current debug
world. It distinguishes proposed player-facing wiring from current source
mechanisms, keeps settings/reset/playback local to each shared station, retains
named presets across sweep banks, preserves Wonderland's player-scale mapping,
and names all six authored table families. The source checker found no
actionable inaccuracy in that handoff.

This review establishes concept and source agreement. The redesigned rooms and
new authored station/link setup are proposed work; no running-World behavior is
claimed by the art review.
