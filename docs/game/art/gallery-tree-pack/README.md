# Gallery tree pack

Eight original landscape paintings propose a warm, handmade future for the Gallery: cream ceramic, honey timber, sage foliage and restrained teal, ochre and coral accents. Dimensions below are modelling targets; the paintings establish form, atmosphere and relationships. [Complete generation prompts](prompts.md) accompany the pack.

## The great tree in the hall

[Whole-tree painting](01-great-tree-hall.png) proposes a living circulation tree whose flattened limbs carry visitors between the ground floor and three upper balcony levels. Canopy skywalks, living-root bridges, pollarded figs and the warm civic woodwork associated with Alvar Aalto inform its branching structure and generous resting places. Carry over 2.2–2.6 metre clear paths, roughly 4 metre junctions, broad bark undersides, continuous exposed-edge guards and teal, ochre or coral edge ribbons that match the destination sockets; keep the canopy airy enough to see the next landing. The nest lookout, lanterns and low swing give the route small discoveries.

## A branch arriving at a balcony

[Balcony joint painting](02-branch-balcony.png) makes the transition from living bark to building deliberate: a broad limb rests in a cream ceramic saddle and opens into a flush, level doorway apron. Real treehouse support collars, canopy-bridge arrivals and rounded boat joinery inform the connection. Preserve a 2.4 metre clear walking width, a 3.6 metre arrival apron, a doorway at least 3 metres clear and approximately 1.1 metre rounded timber guards with rope infill; carry the teal edge cue into the doorway reveal and join the rails continuously to the balcony. Keep the seat and amber lamp outside the walking lane.

## Junctions, steps, rails and canopy

[Tree detail sheet](03-tree-path-details.png) develops a widened Y-junction, a stair-and-ramp pair, woven guards, a trunk-side seat, a nest lookout with a low swing, and broad layered leaves. Living-root forks, playground circulation, furniture joinery and the clear silhouettes of stylized animation backgrounds inform these details. Model level junction pads around 4 metres wide before the 2.4 metre paths diverge; use short runs of approximately 0.12 metre risers and 0.45 metre treads with a continuous sloping alternative. Retain oval timber handrails, cream post shoes, fine sage rope infill and toe curbs; assemble olive-to-lime leaves into airy overlapping fans that preserve destination sightlines.

## Planter and foliage options

[Planting options sheet](04-planter-foliage-options.png) proposes kidney-shaped seat planters, pebble pots, balcony crescents, hanging seedpods and tapered floor vessels, with a small family of lamps, dishes and perches. Mid-century studio pottery, conservatory planting and self-watering public-space planters inform the cream shells, terracotta bases, honey-timber supports and discreet teal inserts. Carry over distinct fern, paddle-leaf, jade, papyrus, arrow-leaf and trailing-coin silhouettes, with coral flowers used sparingly; keep broad vessel lips, visible soil openings, three-point hanging supports and convincing mounting contacts.

## A planted balcony room

[Balcony planting painting](05-planted-balcony.png) places the vessel family around a sunny resting nook while leaving a broad route to the gallery doorway. Planted civic atria, conservatory window gardens and canopy-walk rest stops inform the balance of shelter, foliage and open floor. Preserve a 2.4 metre clear circulation lane, put seating and large paddle plants beside piers, contain trailing growth against planter faces and hang seedpods above occupied headroom. Cream ceramic and warm wood hold the room together; jade foliage, pale coin leaves and small coral flowers provide variety without obscuring the architecture.

## The playroom's woven enclosure

[Netting painting](06-playroom-netting.png) treats the first upper-floor playroom screen as a beautiful textile fitted into the architecture, with an installed view and close studies of its edges. Designed rope playgrounds, maritime braid and woven textile colour fields inform the regular diamond mesh and quiet teal, ochre and coral waves. Retain the full-height timber frame, padded perimeter, rounded mesh door, recessed fasteners and continuous bottom channel; close the mesh against floors, beams and piers, with no loose rope ends or gaps. Keep the public balcony lane outside the enclosure clear and the playroom visible through it.

## A sculpted bouldering pier

[Climbing painting](07-climbing-pier.png) wraps a low climbing face around the base of a structural pier, using softened canyon planes and two readable families of holds. Contemporary bouldering route setting, adventure playgrounds and sandstone fins inform the sand-and-coral panels, teal seams and ochre or teal grips. Carry over a climbing height around 2.5 metres, reachable lateral sequences, distinct hand and foot holds, recessed bolts and a broad sage landing pad extending at least 2 metres beyond the face. Keep waiting explorers and the public route outside the landing area; the smooth pier continues above the climbing face.

## Tall windows and shading fans

[Window painting](08-hall-windows.png) proposes monumental clear glazing with warm timber frames, slender teal mullions, pivoting fan shades and deep planted sitting sills. Brise-soleil shading, greenhouse frame rhythms, folding hand fans and Arts and Crafts window seats inform the design. Preserve the hierarchy of structural piers, broad timber frames and fine secondary bars; use rounded reveals, explicit pivot brackets, overlapping cream-and-timber shade blades and small bronze joints. Give the sill a roughly 0.5 metre sitting depth and keep its planting to the corners, so the canyon view and the walking lane stay open.
