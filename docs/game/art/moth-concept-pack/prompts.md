section gives a kept sheet's inputs, its generation prompt verbatim, and the
corrective edits that followed, one line each. Sheets 05 and 06 and the

scale. Sheets 08 to 13 are crops of the owner's chosen thruster views and the
owner source is the owner's own image; neither has a prompt here.
