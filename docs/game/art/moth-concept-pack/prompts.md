# Moth concept-art prompts

Codex generated every sheet with its built-in image generation tool. Each
section gives a kept sheet's inputs, its generation prompt verbatim, and the
corrective edits that followed, one line each. Sheets 03, 05 and 06 were
finished by scripted compositing that is not kept in the repository. Sheets
08, 09, 12 and 13 are crops of the owner's chosen thruster views and the owner
source is the owner's own image; neither has a prompt here.
