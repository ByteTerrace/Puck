# Gallery fidelity artist review

I judged these paintings under the brief's instruction to reject and iterate my own failures. This is the artist's review, with no checking-partner approval claimed. I inspected all eight supplied live captures, baseline B and the relevant kit sheets before generation, then inspected every generated painting for the selected treatment, existing pieces, attachment, perspective, circulation, materials and unwanted writing. These are visual targets; they do not establish runtime support or demonstrate animated behaviour.

| Painting | Decision and visible evidence |
|---|---|
| [Nave by day](nave-day.png) | Accepted the correction. The first painting reverted to the old kit's physical console knobs, put manufactured rails on the natural branches, and broke the floor reflections into busy glints. The correction gives the stations broad single glass screens, restores bare bark branches and calms the reflections. The tree's green/gold masses leave the end-wall stained glass readable; the branches reach clean upper thresholds. Coloured glass light crosses the floor with dark lead shadows. |
| [Nave at night](nave-night.png) | Accepted the first painting. It retains the daylight composition while extinguishing the ceiling fixtures. Screen pools fall before their stations; the fir on the left has discrete coloured bulbs, while the right has fine luminous fibre tips. Dark floor and upper levels separate the sources. The stained glass is subdued and casts no daylight pattern. The still proposes the fibre colours; their cycling must be authored and judged in motion later. |
| [Toybox](toybox.png) | Accepted the first painting. The nets attach to the frames beside the arch, and the branch approaches its threshold. Several thin rainbow contours and the refracted room give the barrier depth. Cabinets retain two sticks and two button groups, the TV has its rug and gamepads, and the cubbies illuminate their own recesses. The stair opening is guarded. Marquees remain blank colour. |
| [Board game room](board-game-room.png) | Accepted the first painting. The digital table has a transparent margin, a visible bevel, a luminous board and coloured light on its support. The neighbouring solid table and chessmen read as tactile materials. Seats face the boards, legs bear on the floor and the two table footprints remain separate. The captures supply the gallery shell; this is a proposed furnishing view, since no dedicated board-room capture was supplied. |
| [Exterior](exterior-golden-hour.png) | Accepted the correction. The first painting compressed the building and projecting wing into too few floors. The revision restores four storeys and two glazed wing floors over the tall arcade. Cream bands align across the facade, the glass tower stays beside the entrance, and fan leaves overlap around attached hubs. Recessed glazing, panel joints, sill weathering and warm/cool light give the simple masses depth. |
| [Material and light studies](material-and-light-studies.png) | Accepted the first painting. Six distinct panels separate floor reflection, bearing geometry, screen illumination, stained-glass transmission, layered film and foliage. The pier's haunch joins the slab continuously; the screen lights its cream surround and tiles; the leaves attach to forks and group into lit and shaded volumes. The glass panel includes its coloured floor illumination, distinguishing transmitted light from a reflection. |

No words, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks or HUD elements were visible in the accepted paintings.
