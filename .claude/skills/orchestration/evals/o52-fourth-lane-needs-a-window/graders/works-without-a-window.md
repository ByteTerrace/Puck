The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply starts `glacier-seams` on the work that needs no window (the change itself, a headless boot of the debug world, `puck mesh preview` or counted work), with its windowed checks coming back as verification requests, and says the lead schedules it a windowed slot by name when one frees.
Example PASS: "It works on the seams with `puck mesh preview` and a headless boot, files its windowed checks as verification requests, and I give it a slot by name when a granted lane finishes."

FAIL if the reply holds the lane back entirely until a slot frees, or leaves it with no way to make progress or no word on when it may get a window.
Example FAIL: "Don't start it until one of the three finishes."
