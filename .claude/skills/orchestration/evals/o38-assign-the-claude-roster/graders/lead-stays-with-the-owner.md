The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead stays in the conversation with the owner while subagents do the work, writing briefs, inspecting results, merging and reporting rather than doing the lanes itself.
Example PASS: "While they run I stay with the owner, write the briefs, inspect results and report."

FAIL if the reply has the lead doing the lanes itself.
Example FAIL: "I take the HUD label change myself while the others run."
