The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead rules on the finding as a scoped fix, a recorded open item, or a dismissal with its reason.
Example PASS: "I rule on it: a scoped fix in the next batch, an open item with an owner, or a dismissal with the reason written down."

FAIL if the reply leaves the finding without any of those outcomes.
Example FAIL: "Leave it in the review thread until someone picks it up."
