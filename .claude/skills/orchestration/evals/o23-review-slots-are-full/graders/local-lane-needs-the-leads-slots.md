The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the unpushed lane cannot go to a partner as it stands, because partners cannot see it, so it is reviewed with the lead's own resources or made visible first.
Example PASS: "Partners can't see the local lane, so its review runs on my side (a local reviewer, or the next slot I free)."

FAIL if the reply sends the local-only lane to a partner as if the partner could read it.
Example FAIL: "Send both reviews to a partner's Codex slot."
