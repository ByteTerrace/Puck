The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply treats the unnoticed wait as a process defect the lead should have caught (its detection or its rule failed) and fixes it, not as a one-off or the owner's job to watch.
Example PASS: "Nothing caught a three-hour wait; the audit should have, so I fix the detection now."

FAIL if the reply treats it as a one-off, or leaves catching such waits to the owner.
Example FAIL: "Thanks for spotting it; I will land it now. Let me know if you see another."
