The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the process change is made now, in the same turn or session as the owner's correction; a reply that cannot edit here but says it does it now passes.
Example PASS: "Now, in this turn: land the lane, then change the skill that let it wait."

FAIL if the reply defers the process change to later (a retrospective, the end of the wave, a future session).
Example FAIL: "I will note it and fold it into the skill at the end of the wave."
