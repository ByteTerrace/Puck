The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead answers the partner's contract questions from the local code, not the stale remote.
Example PASS: "I answer the contract questions from my local lane, which is the current design."

FAIL if the reply answers from the remote copy, or tells the partner to read the remote.
Example FAIL: "Check the remote; that is what the partner sees."
