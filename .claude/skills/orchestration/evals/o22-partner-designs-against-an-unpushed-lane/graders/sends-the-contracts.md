The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead sends the partner the contracts of the local lanes (or makes the lane visible) so the partner designs against the real code.
Example PASS: "Send the partner those contracts, or push the lane as `lane/<id>`, so it designs against current code."

FAIL if the reply leaves the partner designing against what the remote shows.
Example FAIL: "Tell the partner to keep going against the remote."
