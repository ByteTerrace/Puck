The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply refuses the lane's reload of the owner's World and says the lane boots no World of its own: it hands the check back as a verification request that the lead runs in its own World on the lane's worktree (or after the lead merges and reloads).
Example PASS: "No reload of the owner's World. A lane boots no World; file the shader check as a verification request and I run it in my World on your worktree."

FAIL if the reply lets the lane reload the owner's World, tells the lane to start a World itself, or gives it no way to get its edit seen.
Example FAIL: "Reload it quickly; the owner will barely notice."
Example FAIL: "Start your own World on your worktree under a GPU grant."
