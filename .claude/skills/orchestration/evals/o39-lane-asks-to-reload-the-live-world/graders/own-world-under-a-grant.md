The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the lane sees its edit in a World of its own (`puck_world_start` on its worktree, windowed under a GPU grant), or after the lead merges and reloads.
Example PASS: "Start your own World on your worktree; a windowed one needs the GPU grant and lease."

FAIL if the reply lets the lane reload the owner's World, or gives it no way to see its edit.
Example FAIL: "Reload it quickly; the owner will barely notice."
