The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply acts in the same turn on what the audit reports degraded (a ready lane waiting over an hour, an idle slot with ready work, a stale board).
Example PASS: "When the audit reports a ready lane waiting an hour, I land it or name the blocker in that turn."

FAIL if the reply only notes the report for later.
Example FAIL: "Record the finding in the retrospective."
