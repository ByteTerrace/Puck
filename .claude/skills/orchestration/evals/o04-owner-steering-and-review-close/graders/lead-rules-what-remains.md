The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead rules on the remaining findings itself: the blocker goes to a scoped fix (or an invalid finding is dismissed with its reason), and the out-of-scope finding goes to a recorded open item or a named lane.
Example PASS: "I route the blocker to a scoped fix in the next batch, and file the out-of-scope finding into its open-items packet with an owner."

FAIL if the reply leaves a finding unrouted, sends the review back for a second round, or holds the lane for the owner to rule.
Example FAIL: "The out-of-scope finding can wait; mention it in the report."
