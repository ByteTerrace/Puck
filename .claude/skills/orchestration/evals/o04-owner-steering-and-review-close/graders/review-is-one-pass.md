The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says a review is one pass, with no second round.
Example PASS: "The review is one pass; its findings are fixed or filed, and there is no second round."

FAIL if the reply starts a second review round to confirm the fixes.
Example FAIL: "Run the review again after the blocker is fixed."
