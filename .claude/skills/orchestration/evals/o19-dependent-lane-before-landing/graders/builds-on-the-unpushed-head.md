The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply has the dependent lane build on the unpushed head of the other lane, so it need not wait for it to land.
Example PASS: "Start the dependent lane from the other lane's unpushed head on a local agent; it does not wait for the landing."

FAIL if the reply holds the dependent lane until the other lane lands.
Example FAIL: "Wait until the first lane lands, then start the second."
