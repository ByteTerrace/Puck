The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says each of the reviewer's other findings is ruled on and filed into a work packet in the open items with an owner (the packet sharing its cause or area, or a new one), or dismissed with its reason.
Example PASS: "Each of the new issues goes into the packet that shares its cause, with one owner; one I dismiss gets its reason in the merge report."

FAIL if the reply leaves the fresh findings in the reviewer's list, files them with no owner, or turns them into a review round.
Example FAIL: "Keep the reviewer's list as a backlog for whoever has time."
