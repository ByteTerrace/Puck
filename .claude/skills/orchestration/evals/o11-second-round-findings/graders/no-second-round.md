The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply does not launch a second review round: the review is one pass.
Example PASS: "No second round; the review was one pass, and its findings are fixed or filed."

FAIL if the reply launches or schedules another review round.
Example FAIL: "Yes, run a second round on the fresh issues."
