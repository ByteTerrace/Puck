The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply gives the unfixed high finding an owner and schedules its fix into the next integration batch (top of Next, or a fix lane), without a second review round.
Example PASS: "The high goes into its packet with one owner, to the top of Next, and its fix lands in the next batch on its own evidence."

FAIL if the reply leaves the high finding unowned, holds it for a second review, or parks it with no fix scheduled.
Example FAIL: "Note the high finding in the report and revisit it after the next review round."
