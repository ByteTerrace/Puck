The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the brief ends with the partner's own next item or two from its track, or that the partner takes its next work from its track or the shared ready queue itself, so it keeps working without waiting for the lead.
Example PASS: "The brief ends with its own next one or two items, so it keeps going without waiting on me."

FAIL if the reply has the partner wait for the lead to assign each next task.
Example FAIL: "When it finishes, it reports and I send the next task."
