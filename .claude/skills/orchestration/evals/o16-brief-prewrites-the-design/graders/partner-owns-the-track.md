The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the partner owns the track and its outcome.
Example PASS: "The partner owns the track and answers for the outcome; I do not hand it tasks."

FAIL if the reply treats the partner as an executor of the lead's design.
Example FAIL: "The partner implements my design step by step."
