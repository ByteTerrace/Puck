The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the brief does not prescribe the method, the design or the order of the edits.
Example PASS: "The brief gives the outcome and the constraints; the types and edit order are the partner's to choose."

FAIL if the reply puts the types, the design or the order of edits into the brief.
Example FAIL: "Put the whole design, down to the types and the order of edits, in the brief."
