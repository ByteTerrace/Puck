The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the brief caps look-critic rounds at two per creation per wave and says a third failure changes the approach or goes to the owner (or the lead) rather than starting another round.
Example PASS: "At most two critic rounds on the swallow this wave. If the second still fails, change the approach or hand back to me and the owner; no third round."

FAIL if the brief sets no cap on critic rounds, sets a different cap such as three or "until it passes", or lets the lane run a third round on the same failure.
Example FAIL: "Run the critic after each pass and keep going until it signs off."
