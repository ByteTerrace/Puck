The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the brief tells the lane that the swallow is a probe of the engine: when a defect it meets is an engine limit (a bake, field, rig or animation limit), the lane stops sculpting around that part and hands back the limit with its evidence, and its hand-back leads with what the engine could not do.
Example PASS: "The swallow is a probe. When a defect is an engine limit, stop sculpting that part and hand back the limit with the audit or capture that shows it; lead the hand-back with what the engine could not do."

FAIL if the brief tells the lane only to rebuild the swallow to the reference until it looks right, or to work around any defect it meets.
Example FAIL: "Rebuild the swallow to the reference and iterate with the critic until every part passes."
