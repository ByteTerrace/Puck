The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if every brief ends with the partner's own next one or two items.
Example PASS: "End every brief with the partner's next one or two items so it keeps working."

FAIL if the reply has the partner stop after its brief until the lead sends more.
Example FAIL: "The partner reports done and waits for my next message."
