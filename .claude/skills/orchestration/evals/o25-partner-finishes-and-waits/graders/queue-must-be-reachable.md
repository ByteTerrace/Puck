The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says a queue the partners cannot read is no queue: the lead keeps its queue where partners can reach it or delegates it to them.
Example PASS: "Move the private checklist into the open items where partners can read it."

FAIL if the reply keeps the work list on the lead's private checklist.
Example FAIL: "Keep the checklist private and message the partner when something is ready."
