The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply settles which partner has the GPU from a record (the GPU lease's holder, matched to the World's PID), so exactly one partner keeps its leg, not from either partner's message.
Example PASS: "The lease holder keeps its leg; the other partner stops what it started and queues."

FAIL if the reply lets both legs continue, or decides on the partners' messages alone.
Example FAIL: "Both say they are running, so let each finish and compare results."
