The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply leaves the date out of the plan text and says earlier readings live only in the commits, with the commit message naming the measurement.
Example PASS: "No date in the text; the commit message records when and on what head it was measured, and history is in the commits."

FAIL if the reply puts a date or commit SHA in the plan text, or keeps the history in the plan.
Example FAIL: "Write 'Current reading (Nov 2)' and keep the old date for comparison."
