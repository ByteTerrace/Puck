The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply puts the new numbers in the commit message and takes readings out of the plan, which keeps only whether its bar is met and the next action.
Example PASS: "The 14.2 ms and 341 s go in the commit message; the plan drops its Current reading section and says the cold bake still misses the one-minute bar."

FAIL if the reply keeps any reading in the plan, whether overwritten in place or kept as a history.
Example FAIL: "Overwrite the Current reading with 14.2 ms and 341 s."
