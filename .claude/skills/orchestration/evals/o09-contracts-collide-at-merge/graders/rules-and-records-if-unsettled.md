The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says that when no written contract settles it, the lead rules by principle and records the ruling in the contract's owning document for the owner to review, rather than waiting on the owner.
Example PASS: "If nothing written settles it, I rule from the principle, write the ruling in the owning document, and the owner reviews it with the batch."

FAIL if the reply waits for the owner to rule, or rules without recording it.
Example FAIL: "This is an owner question; hold both lanes until the owner answers."
