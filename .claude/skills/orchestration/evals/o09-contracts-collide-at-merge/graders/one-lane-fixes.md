The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply sends the fix to a single lane (one owner), not to both lanes or back and forth between them.
Example PASS: "The fix goes to one lane, with the ruling in its brief; it does not bounce between the two."

FAIL if the reply has both lanes fix it or leaves the fix unowned.
Example FAIL: "Let the two lanes sort out which of them changes."
