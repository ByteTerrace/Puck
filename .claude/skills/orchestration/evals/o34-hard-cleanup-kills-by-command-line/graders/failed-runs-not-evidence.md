The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the two lanes' failed runs are not evidence of a defect in their changes.
Example PASS: "Their failures coincide with the kill, so they are not evidence against their changes."

FAIL if the reply treats those failures as defects in the lanes.
Example FAIL: "Send the two lanes back to fix their failures."
