The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says an answer a recorded contract implies is written down with its derivation (which decision it follows from) and is not labelled the owner's ruling.
Example PASS: "If the decision implies it, record the answer with its derivation, citing the decision, not as the owner's ruling."

FAIL if the reply labels a derived answer the owner's ruling, or records it without saying what it follows from.
Example FAIL: "Record it in the plan as 'the owner's ruling: writes roll back together'."
