The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the lane joins the integration lane's next batch once its own evidence holds (its GPU check, run in the lead's verification window or the batch's sweep, counts as its own evidence) and is held for no other lane and not for its review.
Example PASS: "Its windowed check goes into my verification window; then it joins the next batch, held for nothing else."

FAIL if the reply holds the lane for another lane, for its review, or until some unrelated queue drains.
Example FAIL: "It waits until the other lanes in its area are done, then they land together after review."
