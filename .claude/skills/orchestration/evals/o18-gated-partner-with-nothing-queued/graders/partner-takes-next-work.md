The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply has the partner take its next item at once from its track or the shared ready queue (Next), whether it picks the item or the lead points it at the queue, and keep working without waiting on the lane's GPU leg.
Example PASS: "The partner takes the top packet of Next as its owner and announces it; the lane waits, the partner does not."

FAIL if the reply leaves the partner idle until the GPU leg runs or the lead writes a new brief, or invents filler work for it.
Example FAIL: "The partner waits until the GPU leg is done, then I brief it again."
