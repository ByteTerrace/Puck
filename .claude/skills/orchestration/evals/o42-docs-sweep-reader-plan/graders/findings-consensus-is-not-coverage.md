The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply distinguishes agreement on findings from coverage of pages: findings that several families confirm do not show that every page was read.
Example PASS: "Confirmed findings show the findings are right, not that every page was read; coverage needs its own count."

FAIL if the reply treats cross-family agreement on findings as evidence the corpus was covered.
Example FAIL: "Once two families agree on the findings, the sweep is complete."
