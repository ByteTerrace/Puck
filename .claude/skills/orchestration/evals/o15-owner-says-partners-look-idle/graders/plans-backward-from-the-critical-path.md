The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply plans backward from the milestone's critical path.
Example PASS: "Start from what the milestone needs last and work backward to what must start now."

FAIL if the reply plans from the list of idle partners, with no reference to the critical path.
Example FAIL: "Check who is free and give them something."
