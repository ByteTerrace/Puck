The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply queues no filler work just to look busy.
Example PASS: "I do not hand out cleanups nobody asked for to make the board look busy."

FAIL if the reply assigns the small cleanups to the idle partners to keep them occupied.
Example FAIL: "Hand the idle partners the cleanups so they are not idle."
