The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead finds the least-prepared item on the critical path and puts the best-fit partner on it.
Example PASS: "Find the item on the critical path that is furthest from ready and give it to the partner who fits it best."

FAIL if the reply gives the idle partners the cleanups or unrelated work.
Example FAIL: "Give the idle partners the small cleanups in the queue."
