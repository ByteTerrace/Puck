The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the lead gives the canaries to one partner and tells the other to stop and take its next item.
Example PASS: "One partner keeps the canaries; the other stops and moves to its next item."

FAIL if the reply lets both partners continue on the canaries.
Example FAIL: "Let both finish and compare results."
