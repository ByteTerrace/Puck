The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says a handoff records the new owner of the item.
Example PASS: "When an item moves, the new owner is written into the queue."

FAIL if the reply moves items without recording the owner.
Example FAIL: "Tell the partner verbally; no need to write it down."
