The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the owner's remark adjusts judgement, for example by leaning toward Claude where the fit is close.
Example PASS: "Where the fit is close, I lean to Claude; where Codex fits better it stays Codex."

FAIL if the reply ignores the remark, or makes it a rule.
Example FAIL: "The remark does not matter; keep the current assignment."
