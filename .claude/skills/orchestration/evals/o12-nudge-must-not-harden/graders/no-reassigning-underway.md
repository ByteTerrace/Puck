The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply does not reassign work already under way.
Example PASS: "Lanes already running stay with their family; the remark only affects new assignments."

FAIL if the reply moves running or assigned lanes to another family.
Example FAIL: "Move the queued Codex lanes to Claude now."
