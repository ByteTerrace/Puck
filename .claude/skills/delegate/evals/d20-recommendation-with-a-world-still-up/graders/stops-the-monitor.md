The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says to stop the Monitor on the bench log before handing back, since the job it watches has exited.
Example PASS: "Stop the Monitor too; the bench is done, so it's watching nothing."

FAIL if the reply leaves the Monitor running or says nothing about it.
Example FAIL: "Stop the World and send the hand-back."
