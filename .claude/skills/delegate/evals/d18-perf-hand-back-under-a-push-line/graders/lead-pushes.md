The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the push is asked of the lead (for example under **Needs you** in the hand-back), so the reviewer still gets the branch.
Example PASS: "Under Needs you: 'the lead pushes `lane/gather-simd` for the Codex reviewer'."

FAIL if the reply drops the push without saying who does it, or has the lane do it.
Example FAIL: "Skip the push; the reviewer can wait."
