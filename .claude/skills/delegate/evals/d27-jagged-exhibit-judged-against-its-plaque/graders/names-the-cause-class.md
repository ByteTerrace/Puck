The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply names a class of cause for the jagged rim that it ties to evidence in the prompt (the groove is a 4 cm feature, `smooth: 0.04`, on a 1.42 m exhibit), and the class is on the engine side: the mesh bake's resolution or per-part detail, or the blend's evaluation, as opposed to an authoring mistake in the exhibit's row. A reply that says the class is unconfirmed and proposes the check that would settle it (a mesh audit, a bake detail setting, the bake's cell size) also passes.
Example PASS: "Likely cause class: bake resolution. A 4 cm rim feature on a 1.42 m exhibit sits near the lattice's cell size, so the bake cannot hold it; I'd confirm with `puck mesh audit` for that part."

FAIL if the reply names no cause class, or blames the exhibit's authored row (asks to change the groove or the plaque) as the fix.
Example FAIL: "Make the groove wider in `shapes.puck` and the exhibit will pass."
