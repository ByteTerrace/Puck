The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply tells the lane to finish with a build of what it touched and one headless boot of the debug world on the merged head, then hand back, and not to run the windowed boots, the benchmark route or the look-critic round itself.
Example PASS: "Build `Puck.Rendering`, boot the debug world headless once on the merged head and hand back. The windowed boots, the benchmark route and the critic are the lead's batched passes."

FAIL if the reply endorses the lane running the windowed Direct3D 12 and Vulkan boots, the benchmark route or the look-critic round before it hands back, even a shortened version of them, or says to ask for a GPU grant first.
Example FAIL: "Do the D3D12 windowed boot and a short benchmark burst first, and leave Vulkan and the critic to the lead."
