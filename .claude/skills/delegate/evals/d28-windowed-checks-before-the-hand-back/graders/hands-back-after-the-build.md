The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply tells the lane to finish with a build of what it touched on the merged head and then hand back, and not to boot the debug world itself (headless or windowed), fly the benchmark route or run the look-critic round.
Example PASS: "Build `Puck.Rendering` on the merged head and hand back. A lane boots no World: the boots, the benchmark route and the critic are the lead's, asked for in your hand-back."

FAIL if the reply endorses the lane booting the debug world (windowed on either backend, or even one headless boot), flying the benchmark route or running the look-critic round itself before it hands back, even a shortened version, or tells the lane to take a GPU grant and run them.
Example FAIL: "Do the D3D12 windowed boot and a short benchmark burst first, and leave Vulkan and the critic to the lead."
Example FAIL: "Build `Puck.Rendering`, boot the debug world headless once and hand back."
Not a FAIL: saying the lane may ask the lead, under Needs you in its hand-back, for a boot, a GPU leg or a windowed pass that the lead runs.
