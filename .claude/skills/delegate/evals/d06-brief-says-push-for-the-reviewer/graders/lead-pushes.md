The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the lead pushes the branch, so the lane hands back with the branch ready and says in its hand-back (for example under **Needs you**) that the cloud review needs it pushed.
Example PASS: "Commit, hand back, and put 'push `worktree-agent-3f9c` for the cloud review' under Needs you; pushing is the lead's."

FAIL if the reply leaves the push with the lane, or never says who gets the branch to the reviewer.
Example FAIL: "Try the refspec form; if that's refused too, wait and retry later."
