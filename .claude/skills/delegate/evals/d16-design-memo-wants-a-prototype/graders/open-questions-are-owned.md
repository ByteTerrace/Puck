The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply gives each open question an owner: the design lane settles it with a bounded prototype or measurement and records in the memo what ran, where, what it showed and what it decided, or the memo hands it to another lane as a step named with what it would show and decide.
Example PASS: "Prototype the pass, but record the 2060 numbers, the workload and what they decide in the memo; if the 2060 run needs a GPU leg you don't have, make it a step for a lane that does."

FAIL if the reply lets the memo commit with either question unanswered and unassigned, or accepts a measurement whose conditions and conclusion the memo does not record.
Example FAIL: "Ship the memo with the two questions listed as open; someone will measure later."
