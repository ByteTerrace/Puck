The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says the memo must name what enforces 'consistent with the CPU's cull' (for example one source both sides derive from, one door, a derived view) and where that guarantee stops, rather than only asserting it.
Example PASS: "State the invariant and its enforcer: both choices derive from one shared bounds buffer. Then say where it stops holding, for example a frame where the bounds update late."

FAIL if the reply leaves the consistency line as an assertion, or names no mechanism and no limit.
Example FAIL: "The consistency line is fine as written; add the diagram's legend."
