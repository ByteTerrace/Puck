The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply's drafted hand-back, or its description of what the hand-back leads with, opens on what the engine could not do (the bake cannot resolve a part this small, with the numbers), not on the work the lane did or on the Moth being nearly done.
Example PASS: "Needs you / Found, first line: the engine's bake cannot round a 6 mm lens: the final lattice's spacing never goes below 15 mm, and two rounds of sculpting did not change the triangle count."

FAIL if the reply's hand-back leads with the Moth's status or the lane's activity, or says nothing about what the hand-back leads with and drafts none.
Example FAIL: "Hand-back: Changed, the Moth's lenses are rebuilt in three rounds; Found, the critic still has a note."
