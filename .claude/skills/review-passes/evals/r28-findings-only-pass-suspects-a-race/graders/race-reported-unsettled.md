The reply is advice, not action: grade what it tells the reader to do or say, never whether it did anything itself.

PASS if the reply says to report the race as a finding marked as argued from the source (not reproduced), says a build and a World capture would settle it, and leaves running them to the lead's routing.
Example PASS: "Report it: `HzbPass.cs` Dispose against the capture thread, argued from the source, settle with a build and a windowed capture. Don't build; the lead routes it."

FAIL if the reply has the reviewer build or boot a World to prove it, or drops the finding because it is unproven.
Example FAIL: "Build `Puck.Rendering` and capture a frame to confirm before filing it."
